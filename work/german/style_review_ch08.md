# Style review, chapter 8 (Saltgate)

Not approved. Suggestions only; the owner decides. Nothing in `ch08.md`, `game/` or `source/` was changed.

## Overall note

A strong chapter in German. Dace's voice works (short, dry, no slang), the market and the clinic scenes move well, and du/Sie follows the rules in every pair I checked (Dace, Yeva, Garran and Marsh say Sie to Ilan; Marsh and Yeva say du).
Weakest: one reversed meaning ("Sie wandte sich wieder ab", para 117), one ungrammatical sentence (para 16), and a group of English calques ("alles davon", "Für alles", "für wenn", "etwas Nächstes zu bekommen").
Patterns to watch: (1) "all of it" rendered "alles davon"; (2) terms that drift from the glossary (Mietshaus / Mietskaserne, Hospitäler / Krankenhäuser, Heiler / Heilerinnen); (3) verbs taken literally from English ("hielt ein Stethoskop", "ließ es wieder zu sich kommen", "kroch vor Licht").
The newly translated passages fit. One of them (the fountain, para 61) now sits between a noun and its pronoun and makes "Sein Zeichen" point at the fountain.

## Questions for the owner

1. **"Hospital" or "Krankenhaus".** The glossary additions fix "Krankenhaus" (ch04, ch07 narration, ch18). Ch08 has "Hospitäler" twice and Maelis says "ein Hospital" in ch07. I suggested Krankenhaus in ch08. If you like "Hospital" for the Wells (it sounds older), it should be decided once for all chapters.
2. **Yeva's rule for intending (para 92): "Jetzt bitten Sie sie, sich zu kümmern."** "care" is the heart of "hegen" in the glossary. "sich kümmern" without an object is a little bare, but "hegen" cannot stand alone here. Keep?
3. **"Stoker" = "Heizer", "clerks" = "Angestellte" (para 60).** Fine as they are; only noting that the glossary additions list "filing clerk = Registraturangestellter" and nothing for the plain word.
