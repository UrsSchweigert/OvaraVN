# Style review, Kapitel 5

## Overall note

A clean chapter with very few meaning problems. The descent into Deep Chorus and Sefa's speech in the hall read well, and Vey's stiff formality comes across (her "Sie" to the two Threaded is right by the rules; Wrack now says "du").
The one grammar error is in para 13 ("den Drachen ... ein langer, heller, gleichmäßiger Ton"). Smaller slips of sense: "Struppiges" for the whiskered hound (para 37), "Er brach sie auf" where he breaks the seal (para 50), "Haken" for a catch in a rhythm (para 48), and "Die ganze Halte singt", which drops the elders' part in it (para 47).
Recurring pattern, as in earlier chapters: English phrases kept word for word ("auf die langsame Art, wie sie es taten", "ein wenig nach dem anderen", "keine Luft, sie darin zu tun", "auf sie einzudenken").
Term checks: "bitten", not "verlangen", for what callers do (para 79); engines are "Motoren" in ch03 and "Maschinen" here (para 20). The eight new passages sit well in their paragraphs.

## Questions for the owner

1. "Dienst" for the paper's "Service" (para 95): decisions.md fixes "indenture" as "Dienstvertrag", so "Dienst" fits. I only ask whether Vey should say "Dienstjahre" to make the bondage clearer on first mention.
2. "die hohe Halle" (paras 33, 72, 74): the glossary proposes "die Hohe Halle" with a capital, as a district name. Here it is lower case, like an ordinary high room. Which is wanted?
3. "Tot im Wasser" (para 16): the English "dead in the water" is a sailor's phrase for a ship that has stopped. The German keeps the words and gains a darker sense. Fine for this scene, I think, but it is a choice.
