## (b) Terms I chose

- Scheitel (crown of a dome), Meeresgrund (sea floor).
- Schweißerfrauen (welders' wives), Gestelle (racks), Werkbänke (benches of stone; Werkbank as in the game's "Werkbank" for Maelis's line).
- Charta kept as in the game text (the terms file proposed Satzung; the owner decision says Charta).

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- "a city built for two": elliptical (two million is meant). Kept as in the game.
- "Slick" is "Schlick" in the game; kept.
- "Wet Market at Saltgate": ch08 places the Wet Market in a city; taken as written.
