## (b) Terms I chose myself

- Pullover (jersey, para 18): the game already uses it in para 144/146.
- Saugnäpfe, Essteller (suckers like dinner plates, para 61): plain words.
- Lederschürze (leather apron), Beete mit Muscheln eingefasst (beds edged with shells), Tanggarten (garden of weed; the game already uses Tanggarten later), Dach aus Platten (roof of slabs).
- Laterne an einem Stiel (lantern on a stalk, small fish, para 5): plain word, same as Laterne for the hospital; no clash in the sentence.
- geflügelt, unten hell und oben dunkel (rays, para 25).
- Everything else in the new passages uses glossary terms (Absatz, Brunnen, Laternenquallen, Lampentang, Königin der Stachelrochen, Heiler, Laterne).

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- Jef says the world will fall dark for "a couple of million people"; Anselm says "forty million people" live above. The two figures do not agree.
