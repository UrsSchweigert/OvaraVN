In a few places the game turned a speech tag into a speaker box ("he observed", "said the old woman", "Okonta said"). I wrote the tags as the usual verb (bemerkte er, sagte er/sie). These are about 15 words in all.

About 105 words new.

## (b) Terms I had to choose

- "Nassmarkt" (Wet Market): from ch08.
- "die unterste Stufe der Planken" (lowest tier of planking): "Stufe" as for tiers in ch08/ch14.
- "Vergnügungsjachten vom Kraterrand" (pleasure yachts off the Rim): Rim = Kraterrand per glossary; "off" read as "from".
- "Tang" (weed): as in Hesper's line in the game.
- "Meerseide" (sea-silk): as in ch12; "Robbenfelle" as in this chapter's game text (ch12 used Seehundfell).
- "heraufgeleitet" (piped up), "behängt" (hung with).

## (c) Possible errors in the English original

- "the bottoms of the stacks" (para 29): "stacks" here means the sea-stacks (Felssäulen), not the Stacks district of Port Halcyon. I read it so, as the game does.
- "Elder Vey has told you that" and "Elder Vey" later: fine, but Vey earlier is called a speaker-elder in ch05; no conflict.
- "Hesper ... 'it wasn't the wrong one. It was only lent.'": the English does not say what "the one" is (gift or hand). Kept open as "das Falsche".
- "I did one of those" (Hesper): "those" is not explained (the Dragon's/ring's kind of order). Kept literal.
- "a hound round a table" etc. not in this chapter; nothing else found.
