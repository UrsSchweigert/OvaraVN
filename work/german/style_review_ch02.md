# Style review, Kapitel 2

## Overall note

A strong chapter in German: the barge scene and the burial keep the author's short, hard sentences, and Hesper's lesson reads naturally. The du forms and all decided terms are used consistently.
Three real meaning errors: "Erntegeräte" for the people harvesting (para 29), "mühelos" for "clean out of the ocean" (para 27), and "Gib ihm nicht unrecht" (para 49), which says the opposite of what Hesper means. "nicht ein einziges Mal" (para 23) reads as "never" and blurs the point about Ione's scars.
Recurring pattern: English shapes kept literally ("wollte ihn weg", "keine erkennbaren Mittel", "ein einziges Ding wollen", "Bist du derjenige?"). Also three forms of "strömen" within three sentences in para 52.
The eight newly translated sentences fit their paragraphs; only "rittlings geritten" next to the new description in para 65 (game text) is clumsy.

## Questions for the owner

1. "Sie war dunkel, grauhaarig und hager" (para 65, new passage): "dunkel" said of a person is unusual in German. The translator kept it open on purpose, because the English "dark" does not say skin or hair. If the art bible fixes Okonta's look, "dunkelhäutig" would read more naturally. Your call, since it must not invent anything.
2. "Geltung bei ihr" for "standing" (para 43): it works, but it is an unusual phrase and it comes back in later chapters. "Ansehen bei ihr" is the more common word. Keep "Geltung"?
3. "Es ist Lastkahn-Saison" (para 10): "Lastkahnzeit" would avoid the loan word "Saison" and the hyphen. Minor.
