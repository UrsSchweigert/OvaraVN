# Style review, chapter 9 (Die Ingenieure)

Not approved. Suggestions only; the owner decides. Nothing in `ch09.md`, `game/` or `source/` was changed.

## Overall note

Good, steady German. Raske is the best voice in the chapter: dry, short, and her Sie to Ilan against his du reads naturally. The storm passage (paras 65 to 70) keeps the pace of the original.
Weakest: pronouns. Three times a German "sie" or "den" points at the wrong noun (paras 40, 47, 54), once with a real change of meaning. There are also a few stiff infinitive constructions ("das zu lehren niemand mehr übrig war", "der gekonnt hätte zu sagen").
Patterns to watch: (1) pronoun reference after a feminine noun; (2) "instandsetzen" written as one word here and as two words in the same chapter and elsewhere; (3) "Inselbewohner" where earlier chapters say "Inselleute".
The new passages (Keelhaven and Lashing descriptions) fit. The Lashing paragraph now has "Da war / Da waren" five times in a row, and "Tauwerk" for both ropes and rigging.

## Questions for the owner

1. **"instandsetzen" or "instand setzen".** Decision 11 writes Jef's craft level as "instandsetzen" (one word). The chapter files use one word in ch09 paras 41 and 79 and ch10 para 77, and two words everywhere else ("instand setzt", "instand setzen", "setzte sie nicht instand"). Duden allows only "instand setzen" / "in Stand setzen" for the verb and "das Instandsetzen" for the noun. I suggested two words. If you want the one-word form as a term of the craft, it should then be one word in every chapter.
2. **"Ältester Oro" after a preposition (para 54: "von Ältester Oro").** Same question as in the ch07 review: "vom Ältesten Oro"?
3. **Para 45, the open quotation.** Raske's speech runs over two paragraphs; para 45 has no closing mark, on purpose (English convention). In ch12 (paras 33 to 34) the same situation was closed and reopened. One rule for the whole book would be good; German books usually also leave the first paragraph open.
4. **"Agravar-Ingenieure" (para 77) and "Agravar-Kind" (ch08).** Hyphenated compounds with the people's name. Fine by me; only checking you want this form rather than "Ingenieure der Agravare" (ch12 has "Ingenieure der Agravare").
