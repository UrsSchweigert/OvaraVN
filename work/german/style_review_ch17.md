# Style review, Kapitel 17 (Standort Neun)

## Overall note

The long vault scene between Ilan, Rook and Maelis is very good German: spare, exact, and Rook's dry voice holds. Du/Sie is correct (Rook, Maelis and Ilan say du; Hanne says Sie).
Most important: three sentences of the original are missing from the German (paras 38, 42, 88). They were cut in the game and were not put back for the book. Two more "must" items are rule slips: "I asked" must be "gebeten" (decision 6), and the gunboat is "sie" although ships are neuter (gender decision).
Patterns to watch: lines that repeat from chapters 15 and 16 but are worded differently here ("Schritt mal/auf zwanzig", "große Last/großes Gewicht", "am/im Gürtel"), "Das habe ich." for "I did.", and "um ... her" for "herum".
Paragraph numbers count from the first paragraph after the heading, scene breaks not counted (135 paragraphs).

## Questions for the owner

1. Para 124: "They were taller than the Spires" is "Sie waren höher als die Türme". In German this says the towers were taller than the towers. If Nine Spires is meant, "höher als die Türme von Nine Spires" would be clear, but the English does not say which Spires. Your call.
2. The six thousand healers are "Heilerinnen" in this chapter (paras 1, 8, 13, 15, 103), but chapters 15 and 16 say "Heiler" for the same group ("jeden Heiler", "Heiler, Tausende"). The glossary makes only the healers of the Hush feminine. Which form for the whole group?
3. "Cupful": "eine Tasse Wasser" (para 9), "Tasse voll" (paras 36, 82, and chapter 16), but "einen Becher voll" in chapter 14, para 70, for the same Saltgate scene. Use "Tasse" everywhere?
4. Para 51: if you keep "Ich habe gefragt", please also check para 74 ("dass jemand fragt"). There "fragt" may be right, because the rays are asked where the cities lie.
5. Para 2: "grey bands on their arms" is "mit grauen Armbändern" (glossary: das graue Armband). A band worn on the arm as a sign is an "Armbinde"; an "Armband" is a bracelet. Is "Armband" wanted?
