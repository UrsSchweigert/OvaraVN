## (b) Terms chosen by me

- "It lay under a brown haze": I wrote "Die Wasserlinie" instead of "Sie", because "Sie" after the preceding "Das waren ... arbeiteten" would be unclear.
- gas flares: Gasfackeln (the game has "Fackeln" for the quay flares)
- "Hesper—" (interrupted speech): "Hesper -" with a plain hyphen, as in the game.
- "Ask. Do not order.": „Bitte“ ... „Befiehl nicht.“ (decision 6)

## (c) Possible errors in the English original

- "Fishing fleets hauled their nets and stood to watch ... the mariners' god go by": Ilan lies on the Dragon's head, but the Dragon is called "it" and "he" in the same stretch ("He was a kilometre long"); this is deliberate (he merges with it), not an error.
- "Hesper—" and "You can't carry—" use em dashes for interruptions; rendered with a hyphen as the owner's rule requires.
- The cut text and the original agree otherwise; no wording difference found.
