# Style review, Kapitel 15 (Das Herz)

## Overall note

A clean chapter with few real errors. The schoolroom dialogue (Garran, Crane) is the best German of the six chapters I read: short, spoken, and each voice is its own. Du/Sie is correct everywhere (Ilan says du; Yeva, Garran, Crane and the guard say Sie).
Weakest: "went down" is translated as "untergehen" (to sink) where the Dragon dives, and a few terms differ from chapter 14 (Landspitze/Ostkap, Hafenmündung/Hafeneinfahrt, Gesellschaften/Konzerne).
Patterns to watch: "er/ihn" that can mean Rook, the ring or the Dragon; Präteritum inside spoken reports where speech would use Perfekt; calques such as "alles von Ihnen", "oder so", "einen Blick tragen".
Paragraph numbers count from the first paragraph after the heading, scene breaks not counted (97 paragraphs).

## Questions for the owner

1. "Seat" (paras 66, 90, 91): "Er sitzt falsch", "Dann setz ihn richtig". The glossary gives "einsetzen" for "seat" and chapter 4 has "falsch eingesetzt". The short form reads well and matches chapter 14 ("Sie saß falsch"). Keep the short form in chapters 14 to 18, or go back to Jef's "eingesetzt"?
2. Para 28: "She had come off her road" is "Sie hatte ihre Straße verlassen". Is "Straße" the word used for a Wellwalker's route in chapter 8? If not, "ihren Weg" would be plainer.
3. Para 28: "fünfzehn andere Brunnenwanderer". The healers of the Hush are a feminine group ("Heilerinnen"). Are the Wellwalkers mixed? If all are women, it should be "Brunnenwanderinnen".
4. Para 39: "the echo's coming back short" is "das Echo kommt zu kurz zurück". Do you read it as shortened (cut off by the dead water) or as coming back too soon? "Zu früh" would be the second reading.
