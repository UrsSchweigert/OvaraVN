## (b) Terms chosen by me

- blades (of the kelp crowns): Blätter
- close tunics: eng anliegende Tuniken
- dull bloom (worn panes): stumpfer Schleier
- shellfish scraped from the dome: Schalentiere (as in the game's later line)
- Kelpstamm (stem of the lamp comparison), langgliedrig, dunkeläugig

## (c) Possible errors in the English original

- Para 5: "the animal ... that had been built by someone fond of dogs" is fine; but "tail-flipper" and "flippers" mix is not an error.
- Para 21/23: Sefa says the holds are "built ... before the Sinking" and later "the sea came up, three thousand years ago"; "Hold Wars, nine hundred years ago" is consistent. No clear error found.
- Para 53: "Hesper ... the only one for two hundred kilometres" while Sefa's mother also intends and "caller from Deep Chorus" exists: not contradictory, only a remark.
- Para 78: "Nobody in any hold had, for six hundred years" vs. Jef's age (older than the Hold Wars, 900 years) is not contradictory.
