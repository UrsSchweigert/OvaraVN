Also changed in form only: "mit dem ablaufenden Wasser an sich" (paragraph 81) is the game wording, kept word for word, although it reads stiffly.

## (b) Terms chosen myself

- "jersey": Pullover. "afterdeck": Achterdeck. "main deck": Hauptdeck. "cabin": Kajüte. "sounding" (a depth measurement): Lotung (not the hold Sounding, which stays).
- "the whole belt of it": den ganzen Streifen (avoids repeating Gürtel).
- "regarded him": musterte. "kind of pleasure": Art Vergnügen.

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- Rook says he had "twenty-two years" with Ilan and also that Ilan sat with him every year "since he was sixteen"; Ilan is about 38, so 22 matches. No error, only noted.
- "It was the cleverest thing Rook had ever offered him": fine. No clear errors found.
