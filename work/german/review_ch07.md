Also new, small: the game drops many dialogue tags (the speaker name carries them). Those were put back from the original ("sagte Okonta", "sagte er", "sagte sie schließlich" and so on), and the closing comma moved outside the German quote mark. This is form, not new content.

## (b) Terms I had to choose

- "gerippt wie ein Gaumen" (ribbed like the roof of a mouth).
- "bepelzt" (furred, of moss).
- "Dach aus Steinplatten" (slab roof).
- "Verwehungen" (drifts of snow on the trench floor).
- "Zinngrau" (pewter).
- "Gewächse" (dried growths), "Dachsparren" (rafters).
- "Krankenhaus" for hospital, as in glossary_additions (ch04). Maelis's "ein Hospital" in the game is kept as spoken.
- "Vorratskammer" for still-room, as in the game (the ch07 terms list said Destillierkammer, the consolidation changed it).
