Also new, but only small: the speech tags that the game leaves out because the speaker box carries them. They are in the file as "sagte er", "sagte sie", "sagte Okonta", "sagte Brask", "sagte Wrack" (paras 3, 9, 14, 18, 20, 27, 32, 34, 38, 41, 53, 57, 58, 60, 72, 83, 86, 92, 95, 102, 110). The tags the game already has were kept. The "he said"/"she said" gender and person follow the English.

The first words of para 40 changed from the game's "Es" to "Er" because the eel (der Aal) now has a full description.

## (b) Terms I chose myself

- Netzhängematten for "hammock-nets" (para 91); the game has Hängematte and Netz separately.
- Kochstein / Kochsteine for "cooking-stone(s)" (paras 56, 91), plain.
- Robbenfell for "sealskin" (para 59); the game already has Robbenspeer.
- Schlammwehe for "a drift of silt" (para 30); Schlamm is the glossary word for silt.
- Bug for "prow" (para 79); Schelf for "shelf" of ice (para 106), same stem as die Hellen Schelfe.
- Geschwüren zerfressen for "ulcered" (para 40).
- "auf der abgewandten Seite" for "on the lee side" (para 56).

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

None certain. Two small doubts:

- Para 63: "The fourth day went by, and the fifth" counts days from the start of the journey, but the stone is reached late on day 3 or 4 and Okonta says "the sixth day from when we started" (para 53). It works if the count is from the start.
- Para 37 and 38: "Twenty years of barges" against bitterwrack "planted years ago" by old women of a hold; readable as consistent.
