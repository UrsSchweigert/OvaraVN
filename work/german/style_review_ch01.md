# Style review, Kapitel 1

## Overall note

The chapter reads well: plain, concrete, close to the author's rhythm, and the invented terms sit naturally. Meaning is almost everywhere exact; I found one real misreading (para 78) and a few small losses of nuance (paras 14, 75).
The weakest spots are word-for-word calques of English constructions ("seit bevor", "Vierhundert von uns", "für das Glück", "es tat es auch") and a few verbs that are slightly off ("ging ... vorbei" for the needle, "lachten ihn aus", "dimmten sich").
The newly translated passages match the game voice. Two of them need a small join fix: para 51 must now say "dem graugrünen Stoff" (the cloth has been shown in para 17), and "ohne ein Zeichen" in para 70 misses the sense of "mark".
Pattern to watch in later chapters: "glühen" for cold light (weed, lamps), where German wants "leuchten".

## Questions for the owner

1. "Ältester Oro" after a preposition or as an object ("sagte sie zu Ältester Oro", para 48): the title is left uninflected, like "zu Doktor Oro". That is defensible for a title, but some readers will expect "zum Ältesten Oro". Is the uninflected form wanted everywhere?
2. "instand setzen" in plain speech (para 56, "Und niemand kann sie instand setzen"): the glossary additions keep "instandsetzen" for Jef's craft level and use "reparieren" or "ausbessern" for plain mending. Sefa does not know the craft levels yet. Keep, or "reparieren" here?
3. "Findest du das fair?" (para 37): "fair" is an English loan in a world with no English. "Scheint dir das gerecht?" would avoid it. Owner's wording, so I only ask.
