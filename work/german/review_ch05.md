## (b) Terms I chose myself

- "tunic": Tunika (as in chapters 1 and 2 of the game).
- "pipes": Rohre. "council hall": Ratshalle (the "high hall" is "hohe Halle" in the game). "inlaid" (floor): "ausgelegt", both times.
- "elders' own chambers": Gemächer der Ältesten.
- "hanging gardens of weed": Hängende Gärten aus Tang (Tang follows Lampentang and Bittertang).
- "trailed": wallten (flowed down).

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- Sefa says "Come quietly" to Wrack and Okonta together; the game uses "Kommt" (see address_ch05.md). Kept.
- The canyon is said to have "hundreds" of tiers, but later the sixtieth tier plus "forty tiers more" gives about 100 in all. "Hundreds" may be loose.
