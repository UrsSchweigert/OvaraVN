# Style review, Kapitel 14 (Die Welle)

## Overall note

A strong chapter in German. The run of the Dragon along the Girdle and the laying down of the wave keep the author's short, plain sentences. Du/Sie is correct, and the fixed lines (Yeva's quote, "Bitte. Befiehl nicht.") match the earlier chapters.
Weakest point: one real meaning error in Hesper's speech (para 48, "We didn't ask for this" is about asking the sea, not about wanting). Second: the Dragon and Ilan are both "er", so paras 14 and 15 need a noun once or twice.
Patterns to watch: "alles davon" for "all of it", loose participles ("sich drehend", "versagend"), "nie ein einziges Mal", and terms that differ from chapter 13 (Aufzug/Fahrstuhl, Wellenbrecher/Mole, bei/in Tidehall).
Paragraph numbers count from the first paragraph after the heading, scene breaks not counted (85 paragraphs).

## Questions for the owner

1. The Rim is "der Kraterrand". In para 24 this gives "an der Kante des Kraters, lag der Kraterrand", which says the same thing twice. Would "der Rand" work as the district's name, or should the sentence become "Und ganz oben, entlang der Kraterlippe, lag der Kraterrand"?
2. "company" is "Firma" in para 72 ("von denen die Firma gesagt hatte"), "Konzern" elsewhere. Same question as in chapter 13.
3. Para 74: "It was seated wrong" is "Sie saß falsch". The glossary gives "einsetzen" for "seat" and chapter 4 has "falsch eingesetzt". Should this be "Sie war falsch eingesetzt", so that the reader hears Jef's word again?
4. Para 70: "a cupful" is "einen Becher voll". If the Saltgate scene in chapter 8 has him hold the water in his hands, "eine Handvoll" may be meant. Please check against chapter 8.
