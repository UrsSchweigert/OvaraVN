## (b) Terms I chose myself

- Pfeiler (pillars of black rock), next to the game's Felssäulen.
- Deckenplatten (ceiling tiles) for "tiles" in the sprinkler paragraph.
- Wappen (crest), as in glossary_additions (Meridian crest = Wappen).
- Muschelschalen (carved shell), Medizin für die Haut (medicines for the skin).
- Springbrunnen (fountain).
- "rose" (face colour) = rosa.
- Bluterguss for "bruised" sky: a plain image, no new fact.

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- "Mr Voss" for Marsh's address: kept as "Herr Voss"; Marsh says Sie to Ilan (rule 2).
- The original ends the chapter with a "---" line before Chapter 9. I did not write it; the lead adds chapter separators.
