## (b) Terms I chose myself

- "ugly brown weed" (para 21): "Tang" (not "Kraut"), so that the next sentence's "Er trinkt Gift" refers to the same masculine word as "Bittertang".
- "dark" (para 65, a woman "dark and grey-haired"): "dunkel". The English could mean dark-skinned or dark-haired; German "dunkel" keeps the same openness.
- "chapped white" (para 38): "weiß aufgesprungen", to match the game's later "aufgesprungenen Hände".
- "rudder, screw" (para 64): "Ruder, Schraube" (ship's propeller).
- "strapping" (para 72): "Verband" (matches Ione's mending of the ribs).
- The ending of the chapter: the original has a "---" before "# Part Two". I left it out; the lead adds the part heading.

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- No clear errors found. One point to note: Hesper is "forty perhaps" (para 38), married nineteen years (para 14) and has netted for twenty-five years (para 58). This is possible (netting from about age 15), not an error.
