## (b) Terms I chose

- the Stacks, "tiers of the Stacks": Stufen der Stapel (glossary: die Stapel; caldera tiers are "Stufen", ch14).
- small desks: kleine Bänke (as the game has it for Garran's line).
- trawler: Trawler.
