# Style review, Kapitel 18 (Meet)

## Overall note

The last chapter is in good shape. The Low Anthem scene and the closing scene in the workshop are calm and exact, and the recurring lines (Maelis's question, "hegen, instandsetzen, erbauen", "Lehre Menschen, die du nicht magst") agree with the earlier chapters. Du/Sie is correct. No sentence of the original is missing.
Weakest: the opening two paragraphs, where the legal and money language is stiff ("fiel nicht so sehr, als dass", "die Gegenleistung ... bezahlen") and Meridian changes gender (es, then ihr).
Patterns to watch: pronouns without a noun ("vor diesen", "Es glich"), English word order in relative clauses (para 16), and generic "you" turned into "dir".
Paragraph numbers count from the first paragraph after the heading, scene breaks not counted (47 paragraphs, the last is "Ende").

## Questions for the owner

1. Para 23: "a city built for two" is "eine Stadt ..., die für zwei gebaut war". In German this reads as "built for two people". "Für zwei Millionen" would be clear. The note in review_ch18.md says it was kept on purpose; do you want the full number?
2. Para 6: "the late chief's intention" is "die Absicht der verstorbenen Chefin". Please confirm that Dr. Marsh is a woman in the source; otherwise "des verstorbenen Chefs".
3. Para 39: "They still read" is "Sie sind noch lesbar". If the meaning is that the walls still play back when touched, "Sie lassen sich noch lesen" is closer.
4. Meridian's gender: "es" in chapters 13 and 18, but "von ihren Rechten" in chapter 15, para 10. Should "Meridian Assurance" be neuter (das Haus) everywhere?
