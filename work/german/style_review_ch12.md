# Style review, chapter 12 (Das Große Moot)

Not approved. Suggestions only; the owner decides. Nothing in `ch12.md`, `game/` or `source/` was changed.

## Overall note

No meaning error found in this chapter. The speeches at the Moot are clear and each speaker sounds different (Hesper hard, Vey formal, Brask blunt, Ione even), and the shared calling keeps its quiet.
Weakest: reference and agreement. Several pronouns or nouns do not match what they point at ("Er" for the tug right after "Er" for Ilan, "einen Sprecher ... Sie war", "eine Wache ... Er war", "Jetzt war sie voll", a "Sie" at the start of a sentence that reads as formal address).
Patterns to watch: (1) "Inselbewohner" four times where earlier chapters say "Inselleute"; (2) echoes that the English sets up and the German drops ("let off" / "let himself off"); (3) the quotation mark rule for a speech that runs over two paragraphs (handled differently from ch09).
The new sentences fit. "vom Salz dunkel verbrannt" has the same problem as in ch11.

## Questions for the owner

1. **"Inselbewohner" or "Inselleute".** The glossary gives "die Inselleute" for the islanders and "Inselbewohner" for one islander. Ch01, ch02, ch05, ch07, ch11, ch17 and ch18 say "Inselleute"; ch09, ch10, ch12 and ch14 say "Inselbewohner" in the plural. I suggested "Inselleute" throughout. Do you want one word?
2. **"ein Offizier der Agravare" (para 28).** Odile reads from the flimsy: "an agravar officer named Ilan Voss". He was a company officer, not a soldier. "Offizier" will be read as military. Is that wanted (the mariners' view), or should it be "ein hoher Beamter der Agravare"?
3. **"niederstrecken" (paras 42 and 77) for "put him down".** The English is the word for killing an animal. "niederstrecken" is a fighter's word. "töten" is plainer; "einschläfern" would be too soft. Keep?
4. **"mit der Ältesten Vey" (para 24).** Here the title is declined with an article; ch07 and ch09 have "an Ältester Oro", "von Ältester Oro". See the ch07 review, question 1.
5. **"Giftmischer" (para 37) for "poisoner".** A "Giftmischer" mixes poisons in secret. Ilan poisoned the holds' water with waste. "Vergifter" is exact but rare. Keep?
