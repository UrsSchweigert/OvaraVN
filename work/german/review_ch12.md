## (b) Terms I chose

- "fishing spear" = Fischspeer; "spectacles" = Brille (der Agravare); "cut-out" (shadow) = Scherenschnitt; "galleries" = Galerien; "steel knives" = Stahlmesser.
- Tug = Schlepper, apron = Kai, tiers = Ränge, bowl = Schale: as in the game and terms_ch12.md.
- Chapter title "Das Große Moot" from strings.rpy.
