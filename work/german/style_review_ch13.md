# Style review, Kapitel 13 (Das Signal)

## Overall note

The chapter reads well. The voice is plain and concrete, and the broadcast scene keeps the short, flat sentences of the original. Du/Sie is correct throughout (Ilan says du, Marit and Raske say Sie, the audience is "euch").
Strongest: Ilan's confession (paras 66 to 74) and Marit's dialogue. Weakest: a few literal calques of English verbs ("go for", "give the speech", "put every screen on you", "came in", "falls") and two wrong material words in the new passages (Torf, Mulde).
Patterns to watch: "die See" in narration (decision: das Meer), "Der Bildschirm zerfiel" (the picture breaks up, not the screen), indicative where reported speech needs Konjunktiv.
Paragraph numbers count from the first paragraph after the heading, scene breaks not counted (92 paragraphs).

## Questions for the owner

1. "lens" is "Linse" seven times (paras 50, 52, 65, 68, 74) but "Objektiv" in para 57 ("mit einem langen Objektiv"). A camera has an "Objektiv"; "in die Linse sehen" is common speech. Keep both, or use "Kamera" for the studio lens?
2. "desk" in Studio One is "Schreibtisch" (paras 31, 50, 78). A studio desk is more a "Tisch" or "Pult". Change?
3. "instand setzen" (para 71, "instand gesetzt") is written as two words here and in most of the book, but decision 11 writes "instandsetzen" and six places in the book use one word. Which spelling should the book use everywhere?
4. "company" is "Firma" in paras 40 and 53 ("Wenn zwei Firmen Krieg führen", "Die Firma hat euch gesagt") but "Konzern" elsewhere (paras 3, 14, 36). Is the difference wanted (corporation = Konzern, company = Firma)?
5. Para 62: "sir" is "mein Herr". The glossary has "Herr?" for "Sir?" in chapter 3. "Reden Sie weiter, Herr Voss." would be plainer. Change?
