## (b) Terms I chose myself

- "cream paper" = cremefarbenes Papier (plain; not "Flugblatt" or "Schuldscheine", since it is stationery).
- "crest" = Wappen (as in ch01 company crest).
- "turf" = Torf; "crag" = Felsen; "fish-loft" = Fischspeicher (as the game).
- "glass wall" = Glaswand.
- No other new terms; all others come from the game translation and glossary.

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- Paragraph 90: Ilan says "twenty-one" for the charges on Low Anthem without saying what the number counts. Kept open; "Ladungen" as in the game.
- Paragraph 26 / 76: Bertil calls Venn "the chairman", but Rook is called chairman of Meridian Assurance (para 85). Venn's title at Orrin is not otherwise given; "der Vorsitzende" is used for both.
- Paragraph 86 says the fleet "sails in two days", while paragraph 11 said five days and the news later says it sailed two days early. Probably consistent with days passing, but not stated.
- Paragraph 3 says the Stubborns lie in the "eastern Girdle" and Ilan "flew over them"; no conflict found, only noted.
