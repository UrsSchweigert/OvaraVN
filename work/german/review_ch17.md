## (b) Terms chosen by me

- wrecks (of ships on the pit floor): gesunkene Schiffe (decision "Ship wrecks next to the name Wrack"), "auf die Seite gekippt" for "keeled over"
- plastered grey: grau überzogen
- fluted: kanneliert
- arcades: Arkaden
- domes of the pale glass: Kuppeln aus dem hellen Glas
- coat (Rook's): Mantel, as Maelis's coat in the game
- "two wheels ... each as wide across as a table": Jedes war im Durchmesser so breit wie ein Tisch
- "Spires" kept as "die Türme" (as the game; probably Nine Spires, left unnamed)

## (c) Possible errors in the English original

- "the Spires" (para 130, "taller than the Spires"): probably the Nine Spires, but the text does not say.
- Ilan "I've come to seat it" / "No. Right.": the wordplay on "seat it right" is lost in German (as in the game).
- "Tuesday": the English text has Rook open the seam "on a Tuesday" after 49 years; Ilan repeats "on Tuesday". No error, only noted.
- The speaker of "I've been promised it too, lads" is Garran in the English text and in the game (owner decision); the paragraph follows this.
