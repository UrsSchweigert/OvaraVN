## (b) Terms I chose myself

- "wrecks of twelve ships" = "Überreste von zwölf Schiffen" (as in the game; "Wracks" would clash with the name Wrack).
- "burned dark" = "dunkel gebrannt" (plain; the English does not say by what).
- "kelp stems" = "Kelpstämme" (as ch01 "Stämme" for the kelp trunks).
- "lashed in strings" = "in Strängen verzurrt" (plain; "Stränge" is used in the glossary additions for skeins, so "Fäden" is avoided).
- "capable (face)" = "tüchtig".
- Everything else follows the game translation, the glossary and docs/german/work/terms_ch11.md.

## (c) Possible errors in the English original (not fixed)

- Para 65: "The boy looked up at her large dark eyes" (sic): the boy looks up at her, not at her eyes. I kept the odd wording as "zu ihren großen dunklen Augen auf". The game text had already cut it to "looked up at her".
- Para 57: "It could not have him. So it took what was holding him." The subject "it" is never named (the cold or the sea). Kept as "es".
- Para 5: "I know the hull" (the youth knows the pilot by his ship); the English is unclear. Kept literal, as in the game.
- Para 11 (spoken line): "the third line of the paper": unclear whether the contract or the purple notice (already open in docs/german/consolidation.md). Kept "Papier".
- Para 34 and 66: "the woman from Fallow thrown down" and later "the woman with the tired face" rescuing sailors: the same woman, who is thrown down and then walks up unhurt. Not a contradiction, only unremarked.
