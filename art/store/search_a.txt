Anime-style key art, clean dark line art, soft cel shading, cinematic, tall vertical poster composition. A lean adult man with short messy black hair and a plain grey-green tunic, seen from behind and slightly to the side, standing at the bottom of the frame on the sea floor, one hand raised, pale light in his palm. Rising above him, filling the tall frame to the top, the enormous Dragon: a kilometre of pale metal ribs, hoop after hoop, bright glowing water bound to the ribs streaming like a flame, curving up and away through deep blue water with shafts of light. A silver needle-craft with a soft light along its flank rises beside him. A sense of vast power and awe. No text, no letters, empty dark water at the top third of the frame.
