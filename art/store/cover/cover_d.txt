Anime-style key art, clean dark line art, soft cel shading, cinematic, tall vertical book-cover composition. Central subject, filling the middle of the frame: the enormous Dragon, a kilometre of pale metal ribs, hoop after hoop, in a great S-curve, with bright glowing pale blue and white water bound to the ribs and streaming along the spine like a cold flame; no fire, no orange, no yellow flame, exactly the colours and shape of the Dragon in the first reference image. Across the top of the frame, above the sea surface, the dark silhouettes of crowded island cities of towers and chimneys, lightless except for a few small windows, under a sooty olive-brown sky with black smoke. Thick black sludge and oily dark waste pours from the islands into the sea and sinks in long dark streaks and plumes through the upper water, with grey rubbish falling like slow snow. The water is deep clear blue around the Dragon. At the bottom corners of the frame on the sea floor, a few holds: hill-sized domes of pale smooth panes with seamless white ribs, set directly on the sand, as in the second reference image; one dome glows warm yellow-green, the other is dark and failing; a little kelp. Between them, small, a lean man with short messy black hair and a plain grey-green tunic, seen from behind, one hand raised with a pale light in his palm. No needle-shaped craft, no ships, no brick or stone walls, no text, no letters, no watermark.
