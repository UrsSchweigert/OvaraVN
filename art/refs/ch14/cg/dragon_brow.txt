Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no readable marks. Eyes fully drawn with irises, pupils and highlights. Reference 1 is Ilan, a lean man with light skin and short messy black hair, in a plain grey-green tunic. Reference 2 shows the design of the Dragon and only that: a long smooth pale metal ship-bow-shaped head with no eyes, no mouth, no fins, and behind it a long straight row of tall upright hoop ribs along a spine. Reference 3 is the open sea at sunrise after rain. Wide view from low over the water at sunrise, rain squalls far off. The Dragon has surfaced and floats ON the sea: in the right foreground its huge smooth pale metal head, as long as a big ship, riding the swell with white water breaking round it; behind it a very long straight row of enormous hoop ribs, each taller than a house, rising out of the swell and receding to the far horizon, a kilometre long, each rib trailing a long mane of white spray, the water bound inside blazing bright white-gold in the early light. Exactly one tiny man, Ilan, lies flat and long along the top of the head, small against it (his whole body a tenth of the head length), both palms pressed flat on the warm metal, his face turned toward the ribs ahead, calm. Nobody else, no needles, no ships.
