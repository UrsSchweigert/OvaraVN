Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no readable marks. Reference 1 shows the design of the Dragon and only that: a smooth blunt pale metal hull-shaped head at the front with no eyes, no mouth, no teeth, no horns, then a long straight row of tall upright hoop ribs along a spine. It is not a creature with a face. Seen from high above at sunrise, the sea a grey-green swell with rain squalls. The Dragon floats ON the surface and runs along the horizon: the smooth blunt hull-head ahead, and behind it a line of ribs a kilometre long rising out of the swell, each rib trailing a long mane of white spray, the water bound inside the ribs blazing bright white-gold, the sea parting white round the head. Beside its head, small, runs one slim pale needle, a narrow torpedo-shaped vessel pointed at both ends with no windows, Okonta, a woman with dark brown skin and short grey hair in a slate-grey suit, lying flat on it, the only dark-skinned rider. Behind and around, small pale needles, about fifteen, skim out of the grey rain squalls one by one to join, each tiny with a rider lying on it. The Dragon is the huge subject; the figures are tiny.
