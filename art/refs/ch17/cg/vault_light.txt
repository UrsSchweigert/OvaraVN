Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no readable marks. Eyes fully drawn with irises, pupils and highlights. Reference 1 is Ilan, 2 is Rook, 3 is Maelis, 4 is the vault. Exactly three people in the round vault whose walls have turned to soft white light with faint moving pictures of white towers and steps: Ilan is a lean man with light skin, a thin pale line on the side of his neck and short messy black hair, in a plain grey-green sea-silk tunic. Ilan kneels on the stone at the rim of the huge metal ring in the floor, one palm flat on the metal, his other arm stretched out across the ring holding out a small tool handle-first. On the far side of the ring an extremely old man, Rook, with thin white hair, very pale papery skin, deep lines, a pale shirt with an open collar and hands wrapped in stained rags, sits at a plain bench with drawings round it, looking at Ilan. Standing on the bottom step of the stair is Maelis, an old woman with very short grey hair and light skin, in a dark patched coat, hands in her pockets, watching. Nobody else.
