Eyes fully drawn with irises, pupils and highlights. Reference 1 shows the tank-town: concrete troughs behind a sea-wall with hanging white lamps and a broken open sluice gateway to the harbour. Late afternoon, low warm light. Seen from very high above and far away, so that every figure is small, only about as tall as a fingertip: a long thin line of about forty tiny grown adults, tall thin men and women of mixed ages with dark or grey hair, in plain grey-green tunics, climbing up out of the pale still water of the concrete troughs on the left and walking one behind another in single file along the wall-top toward the wide open gateway in the sea-wall, and beyond the gateway a loose group of the first of them already standing waist-deep in the clean calm harbour water outside, faces lifted to the sky. Wide establishing view, mostly the tank-town, the wall and the sea; the people are small. Quiet, solemn, release. No children.
