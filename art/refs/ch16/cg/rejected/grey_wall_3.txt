Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no readable marks. Eyes fully drawn with irises, pupils and highlights. Reference 1 is the sea of the Girdle with thousands of boats and a long flat grey wall on the western horizon. Same wall, same boats, now seen from a low viewpoint at the sea surface. In the nearest water, about two hundred mariners hang in the warm milky grey sea with only their heads and shoulders above the surface, seen from behind and from the side, long-limbed and thin, dark wet hair, large dark eyes, light to brown skin, all looking west in perfect silence, drawn up in a long uneven line along a sharp straight dividing line where the water ahead of them turns flat, dark and glassy. Beyond that line, a very long way off, the flat-topped grey wall stands a little higher than the sea like the edge of a table with mist lying along its top, and a faint ripple runs along its whole length as if it has just stepped nearer. Boats and rafts crowd the water behind the mariners. Held breath, awe, quiet.
