Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, no text, no letters, no readable marks. Eyes fully drawn with irises, pupils and highlights. Reference 1 is the sea of the Girdle with thousands of boats and a long flat grey wall on the western horizon. Same wall, same boats, same pale grey sky, now seen from the high rail of a boat looking out over the water. Below and ahead, about eighty grown adult mariners hang in the milky grey sea in loose rows, seen small and from behind, only their heads and shoulders above the water, all fully dressed in dark and pale sea-silk robes with high collars, dark wet hair and grey hair, adult proportions, all looking west in perfect silence. They are drawn up along a sharp straight line in the water where the milky warm-looking sea ahead of them changes to dull flat grey glassy water, a sharp line across the surface like a shoreline, no cliff and no drop, the water level the same on both sides, nothing black. Far beyond that line the flat-topped grey wall stands a little higher than the sea like the edge of a table, with mist lying along its top and a faint ripple along its whole length as if it has just stepped nearer. Boats and rafts crowd the water behind and beside the mariners. Held breath, awe, quiet.
