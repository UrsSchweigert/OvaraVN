Anime-style wide illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, cinematic composition, restrained and quiet, no text, no letters. Reference 1 is Maelis, a tall straight woman in her seventies with grey hair in a low bun and a patched dark charcoal coat. Reference 2 is Jef, a very old, slight, nearly bald man with white tufts over the ears, in a brown leather apron over a worn wool jersey, eyes closed. Reference 3 is the landing at dusk: red twilight at the top of the world, black still water, snow and ice. Exactly two people. Maelis stands on the dark stone landing holding Jef up in both arms against her chest and shoulder. Jef is a FULL-SIZED old man, not tiny: his body is the length of her whole torso and hips, his head rests on her shoulder, his eyes are closed and his face is peaceful and still, one arm and both legs hanging down, his boots reaching her knees. He is clearly an adult old man lying in her arms, as an adult carries another adult. Her face is composed, grief held quietly, looking out over the water. Beside the landing the huge pale ribbed machine, the Dragon, lies intact and calm on the black water, long tall pale ribs along a straight spine, unharmed, and along its whole length a faint cold BLUE light glows softly from within it, a pale blue glow shining through the ribs and reflecting on the water. Over the water beside it huge dark stingrays rise and fly through the air, wing beside wing, like an escort of sails. No other people.
