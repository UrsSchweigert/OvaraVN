Anime-style background illustration, clean line art, soft cel shading, gentle natural colours, wide landscape view, no people, no text, no letters. ENTIRELY UNDERWATER, looking along the wall of a vast canyon city built in tiers down both walls, no sky. Gallery after gallery of pale stone and glass terraces and windows, all the Maker lamps dark and grey, descending far down into deep blue. In place of lamps, long swags of weak greenish-yellow glowing weed hang from the galleries and rails, dim and sickly, the only light. Below, the blue depths fall away. Cold, tired, hushed, mournful.
