# Brief: the full German text of the novel (for the German ebook)

Goal: a complete German version of ONE chapter of the ORIGINAL novel (source/novel.md), as running book prose, for an EPUB. This is NOT the game script. Output: work/german/chNN.md (NN = chapter number, two digits), in the same markdown structure as source/novel.md.

## Inputs (read completely)
- The English original chapter: source/novel.md (find '## Chapter N.' and read to the next chapter heading). Read-only. Never edit it.
- The approved game text of the chapter (what remains after the owner's cuts): work/cut/chNN.md, and its German translation, the game translation: game/game/tl/german/chNN.rpy (English line in the comment, German below; the box order follows work/cut/chNN.md and game/game/chNN.rpy).
- Binding decisions and terms: docs/german/decisions.md, docs/german/glossary.md, docs/german/glossary_additions.md, docs/german/address.md, docs/german/work/address_chNN.md and terms_chNN.md for your chapter, docs/german/consolidation.md.

## Method
1. Compare the original chapter with work/cut/chNN.md paragraph by paragraph and sentence by sentence and list (for yourself) what the owner cut or replaced. The game text is the original minus cuts; some sentences may have been replaced by the owner's approved wording (a `replace` that reuses the author's words). For the EBOOK use the ORIGINAL English wording everywhere.
2. For every passage that the game contains unchanged: reuse the German of the game translation word for word (it is the owner's reviewed and decided wording), only adapting the FORM for running prose: join the boxes of one paragraph into one paragraph; put dialogue in German quotation marks „…“ and ‚…‘ for quotes inside quotes (the game shows speech without quotes because the speaker name box carries it); the comma or full stop of a spoken line that continues with a tag goes OUTSIDE the closing mark in German ("Fass nichts an“, sagte Sefa. / „Komm“, sagte sie.), but ?, ! stay inside and then no comma follows ("Kommst du?“, fragte sie.); rebuild sentences that the game split over boxes; keep italics as *...* exactly where the original has italics.
3. For every passage that the game cut (or where the original wording differs from the game wording): translate it NEW from the original English, in the same voice and with the same terms, names and du/Sie rules as the game translation (docs/german/decisions.md is binding: Wrack kept, die Halte, Meerleute/Agravar, Faden/Fadenträger, rufen/Rufer/hegen, Heger/Hegerin and hegen/Hege, Ältester/Älteste as title before the name, das Moot, Plagegeist, Meet stays English, Land for ground, Schuldscheine/Papier/Flugblatt by sense, Dienstvertrag, Charta, Flutsaum, Schleusenkahn, Vorratskammer, die Bleiche, die Hellen Schelfe; du/Sie by the final rule 1-5 and 4b/4c/4d; Präteritum; sea = das Meer in narration and die See when a mariner speaks of it as a person; Dragon = der Drache; no dialect). Where a cut passage contains something the game translation never translated (new names, objects), take the term from the glossary or choose the plainest German and list it in your report.
4. Keep the structure EXACTLY: one German paragraph for every English paragraph of the original (same count, same order), scene breaks '---' where the original has them, the chapter heading and the closing '*Ende*' only where the original has '*The End*'. Do not merge or split paragraphs, do not skip any, do not add anything, do not add explanations or translator's notes inside the text.
5. Typography: NO en dash and NO em dash anywhere (the owner's rule): where the English has an em dash use a comma, colon, full stop or, for an interrupted or trailing-off speech, a plain hyphen with the German spacing (Ich wollte nicht -); „…“ for speech; ‚…‘ inside; ellipsis as … ; numbers and units as in the game translation (metric).
6. Heading format in the file: first line `## Kapitel N. Titel` with N the chapter number and Titel the German chapter title from docs/german/glossary.md section F / game/game/tl/german/strings.rpy (chapter 18 keeps "Meet"). Do NOT write part headings (the lead adds them). One blank line between paragraphs.

## Deliverables
1. work/german/chNN.md (the chapter).
2. work/german/review_chNN.md: (a) a table of EVERY passage you translated NEW (cut or replaced in the game): original paragraph number (count of paragraphs in the chapter), first words of the English, the German you wrote, so the owner can review the new translation; (b) the glossary terms you had to choose yourself; (c) possible errors in the English original (not fixed).
3. Run a self-check with standard-library Python (write the script into the scratchpad directory, not into the repo, or reuse tools/check_translation.py ideas): paragraph count equals the original's count; no en/em dashes; no straight double quotes; balanced „ “; every '*' italic span balanced; each game German line's words appear in your chapter (spot-check 30 random lines). Fix every finding.

## Rules
Read CLAUDE.md first (source/ read-only; never edit decisions.json; no secrets; do not commit; do not push). Touch only your own two files (work/german/chNN.md and work/german/review_chNN.md). Do not run the game and do not run renpy.

## Report (max 250 words, plain)
Paragraph count (original / yours); number of passages translated new (paragraphs and approximate words); the hardest choices (max 6); du/Sie doubts (max 4); possible errors in the original; what you could not verify. Do not claim approval: only the owner approves.
