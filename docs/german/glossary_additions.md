# German glossary: additions from the chapter translations

Status: for the owner to judge, like the glossary. Nothing here is approved. These are the terms the chapter translators had to choose that are not in `glossary.md` or `decisions.md`. The German shown is what is now in the game files after the consolidation pass (`consolidation.md`). Mark each row Accept, Change to ..., or Discuss. "ch" means the chapter where the term first matters; "same" means the chapters agree.
