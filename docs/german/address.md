# du / Sie: who says what to whom (chapters 2 and 6)

Status: working table for the owner to confirm. Nothing here is approved.

## The rule (owner, final version, see `decisions.md`)

1. A Threaded person (Fadenträger) says **du** to everyone.
2. A person who is **not Threaded** says **Sie** to a Threaded person, whatever their ages.
3. Between two people who are both not Threaded: the younger says Sie to the older, the older says du to the younger, people of about the same age say du, children are du.
4. **Sefa, Wrack and Ione** say du to each other, both ways, in every chapter.
5. Wrack counts as Threaded from the chapter 4 scene where he receives the Thread; before that he is not Threaded.

Working readings (please confirm): "about the same age" = no clear age gap in the text; a gift is no longer part of the test.
