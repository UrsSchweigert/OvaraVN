# Terms for chapter 1

## Terms not in the glossary or decisions

| English | German | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| kelp frond | Kelpwedel | "Wedel" is the plain word for a frond |
| harvesting hook | Erntehaken | plain compound |
| nursery terrace | Aufzuchtterrasse | the nursery is for young fish, so not "Kinderterrasse" |
| drying racks | Trockengestelle | plain |
| the Hold Wars | die Haltekriege | glossary proposed "Hortkriege", but the owner chose "Halte" |
| meeting hall | Versammlungshalle | plain |
| tail-flipper | Schwanzflosse | plain |
| Tidecaller (Hesper) | die Flutruferin | glossary "Flutrufer", female form |
| air-hearts that make the dry rooms dry | Luftherzen, die die trockenen Räume trocken machen | "rooms" kept as in the English (not "Hallen") |
| mind your head | gib auf deinen Kopf acht | plain |
| "we just keep them" (holds) | Wir bewahren sie nur | avoids a pun on "Halte/halten"; "hüten" is used for herding fish and for the Threaded keeping waters |
| "Wrack, what we call what the tide brings in" | So nennen wir das Strandgut, das die Flut bringt | owner decision 1: Strandgut (Treibgut is the other option) |
| "mend" (a lamp) | instand setzen | glossary craft term; used in Sefa's and the narrator's lines |
| "tended" (the forest) | gehegt | decision 11; note the overlap with "gehegtes Wasser" |
| "It mends things" (healer water) | Es heilt | glossary: heilen for the healer |
| "Mother's way" | Muttersart | short and plain; no glossary entry |
