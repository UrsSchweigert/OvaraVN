## Where Wrack receives the Thread

The Thread is laid in scene s09, in the night after "Come up when the sun's at the notch". The text then says "He woke in the box bed by the window, and the world had a second edge to it." (box 373 in the table). Per rule 5, Wrack is Threaded from that sentence on. Everything before it (boxes 1 to 372) has him not Threaded; everything after (boxes 373 to 441) has him Threaded.
