# Brief for chapter translators (German version)

You translate ONE chapter. The generated stub file `game/game/tl/german/chNN.rpy` already exists (generated by Ren'Py; ids are final; ids depend on speaker and English text, so never edit the English). Fill the German text into the existing blocks in place (each block has the English line as a comment and the English line to replace; ch02.rpy and ch06.rpy are finished samples: copy their conventions).

Read first, completely: docs/german/decisions.md (binding), docs/german/glossary.md (proposals), docs/german/address.md (who says du/Sie; build the tables for your chapter's speakers in docs/german/work/address_chNN.md), docs/german/sample_ch02.md, docs/german/sample_ch06.md, the English work/cut/chNN.md and game/game/chNN.rpy, game/game/tl/german/ch02.rpy, ch06.rpy, strings.rpy. Use the glossary's PROPOSED German for unmarked rows.

Rules: published-translation quality, the author's voice (plain, restrained, concrete), no calques; Präteritum; du/Sie by the final rule in decisions.md (Threaded say du to everyone; non-Threaded always Sie to Threaded; among non-Threaded the younger says Sie to the older; Sefa/Wrack/Ione and Okonta/Wrack always du; check in the text who is Threaded and from when, and who is how old); „…“ and ‚…‘ for quotes; NO en dash and NO em dash anywhere (plain hyphen only if a break is really needed); keep every {i}/{/i} and other tags and [interpolations] exactly; metric units; add and remove nothing, no new facts; one German line per English line (the script splits sentences over boxes: follow the split). Keep German lines under about 420 characters where the English is under about 380.

Do NOT touch any file outside this list (other agents work on other chapters in parallel): game/game/tl/german/chNN.rpy (yours), docs/german/work/*_chNN.md and docs/german/review/chNN.md (yours). Do not edit strings.rpy, glossary.md, decisions.md, address.md, tools, the English scripts, or any decisions.json. Do not run `renpy.sh game translate`, do not run the game (the lead runs smoke tests later), do not commit, do not push, no secrets.

Deliverables
1. The finished game/game/tl/german/chNN.rpy.
2. docs/german/work/strings_chNN.md: every NEW speaker label, chapter title, sign text or other string in your chapter that needs a `translate german strings` entry and is not in strings.rpy already (old English | new German), so the lead can merge them (duplicate `old` strings across files break Ren'Py).
3. docs/german/work/terms_chNN.md: every term you had to render that is NOT in the glossary or decisions (English | German | reason), and every glossary term you used that the owner has not confirmed.
4. docs/german/work/address_chNN.md: speaker table (Threaded yes/no, age and source, gift) and the pair table (who says du/Sie to whom, and why); mark uncertain pairs.
5. docs/german/review/chNN.md: the side-by-side table (number | line in chNN.rpy | speaker | English | German) in the style of sample_ch06.md.
6. Run `python3 tools/check_translation.py N` (N = chapter number) and fix every flag (shorten without dropping a fact). Re-read the whole German chapter once for terms, tense, du/Sie and rhythm, and fix.

Final report (max 250 words, plain): lines translated, checker result, longest German vs English line, the hardest sentences and choices (max 8), uncertain du/Sie pairs (max 8), possible errors you noticed in the English source (do not fix them), and anything you could not verify. Do not claim approval: only the owner approves.
