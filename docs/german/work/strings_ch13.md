# New strings, chapter 13

| Old English | New German |
|---|---|
| Captain | Hauptmann |

All other speaker labels are names and stay unchanged (Ilan, Sefa, Okonta, Dace, Raske, Marit, Caspar Venn, Bertil). "Captain" is the label of the Meridian captain (c_captain); glossary group G proposes "Hauptmann". Check strings.rpy for a duplicate. The chapter title "The Signal" ("Das Signal") is not shown by the script; if the chapter menu needs it: The Signal | Das Signal. No sign texts need strings entries: the two screen captions (VOSS, FORMERLY OF HALCYON ... and PORT HALCYON ...) are inside normal text boxes and are translated there, in capitals.
