## Choices to look at

- "Silence" (the sea going quiet) is **Lautlosigkeit**, not "Stille", because "die Stille" is already the south region.
- "harvesters" is **Erntegeräte**; the English does not say whether these are tools or people.
- "standing" in Hesper's three things is **Geltung** ("du brauchst Geltung bei ihr").
- "I didn't mean-" and "I have to-" become "Ich wollte nicht -" and "Ich muss -" with a plain hyphen.
- "No caller in the songs" is "Kein Rufer aus den Liedern".
- "Say it back" is "Sprich es nach".
- "sludge" is **Schlamm** (not "Schlick", which is the dead zone).
- The sea: Hesper says "die See" and "sie" (a person); the narration says "das Meer".
